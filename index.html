About

I am a designer at heart and a problem solver by instinct. I enjoy understanding the why behind things, challenging the obvious, and turning complex problems into simple, meaningful experiences.

I believe good design is not just about how something looks, it is about how thoughtfully it works, how naturally it fits into people's lives, and how it makes them feel.

Curious by nature and detail oriented by choice, I constantly explore the intersection of design, technology, people, and business. I like ideas that are purposeful, products that feel effortless, and solutions that have a point of view.

I lead a design team through every phase of the process, from understanding consumer trends to translating them into products that hold up on the shop floor and in the factory. I care about aligning user centric solutions with business goals, and about building a culture of design excellence within the team I lead.

Thoughtful

I don't just design things. I think about why they should exist, who they are for, and how they can be better.
